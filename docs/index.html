HSB-Device

Experiments, builds and notes from a personal Android device lab.

A personal lab around one Android device: kernels, builds, experiments, and whatever comes out of them.

Repository

  • LanceX — Linux 4.9.337 kernel for the Realme 5 Pro (RMX1971 / Snapdragon 712), with KernelSU and Simple LMK support.
  • AnyKernel3 — AnyKernel3 flashable packaging for RMX1971 kernel builds.
  • Kernel-Builder — CI pipeline that cross-compiles LanceX with proton-clang and publishes AnyKernel3 artifacts.

Philosophy

  • Hacky over standard when it buys speed, size, or understanding.
  • Read the source, then prove it on the device — no vibes.
  • Every claim is scoped to a configuration and backed by a measurement.
